Hello! My name is Joel Ndiba Mwaura, and I am an educator, technology enthusiast, and lifelong learner from Eldoret, Kenya.

My journey has been shaped by education, perseverance, creativity, and a desire to make a meaningful contribution to society. I hold a Bachelor of Education (Science) from Moi University and an Associate of Applied Science in Applied Technology from Brigham Young University–Idaho. I have also pursued certificates in Computer Programming, Web Development, and Web Frontend Development.

Over the years, I have gained valuable experience in education, including serving as Deputy Headteacher at Neema Christian School in Eldoret. Working with learners has strengthened my communication, leadership, teamwork, and problem-solving abilities. It has also taught me the importance of patience, creativity, and making knowledge accessible to others.
